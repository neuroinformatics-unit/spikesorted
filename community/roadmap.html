Roadmap#

This page highlights the upcoming plans for spikesorted, including additional pipeline steps and features to streamline running the pipeline.

Planned pipeline steps#

We plan to add three additional (optional) steps to the pipeline: artefact curation, unit matching, and curation.

  • Save data in NWB format.

  • Support multi-session alignment.

Planned features#

  • Support multi-session alignment.

  • automated worked allocation (n_jobs) based on available memory.
